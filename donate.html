Blood Donation FAQs & Eligibility

Everything you need to know before visiting a donation center.

Healthy whole blood donors can donate once every 56 days (8 weeks) for men, and every 12 weeks for women. Platelet donors can donate as often as every 7 days, up to 24 times a year.

If your tattoo or piercing was applied at a state-regulated, sterile medical facility, you may be eligible immediately or after 3 months depending on local health department regulations.
