Give Blood, Save Lives

A voluntary blood donation occurs when a generous individual gives whole blood or specific components (platelets, plasma) to support patients undergoing surgery, cancer treatment, or trauma care. Join our healthcare mission to ensure safe blood is accessible to every life in need.
